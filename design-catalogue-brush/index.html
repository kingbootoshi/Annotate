Brush Cursor Catalogue II

Five brush identities. Draw in each one - the ink dot at the tip is always your exact stroke width. Tell me the number (or a mix).

01 · Round Sable

The classic artist brush. Full chestnut tuft, brass ferrule with crimps, tapered dark handle.

elegant · traditional

02 · Flat Wash

Wide chisel bristles with visible strands, silver ferrule, stout handle. Feels like a real wash brush.

bold · wide

03 · Ink Dip

Round sable whose tip is dipped in your current color. Switch swatches and the bristles drink it.

color-reactive

04 · Fude

Japanese calligraphy brush. Long bamboo handle with joints, white bristle body, inked black tip.

minimal · precise

05 · Cartoon Chunk

Fat playful brush with thick black outlines, blue barrel handle, gold ferrule. Reads instantly at any size.

playful · loud
