Toolbar Glass Catalogue

Five live directions for the Annotate shortcut bar — hover, click tools, feel the springs. Pick one (or a mix) and it gets ported into the app.

01

Liquid Glass Capsule

One continuous frosted capsule, macOS-26 style: specular top edge, heavy blur+saturation, a glass lens that glides to the active tool. The most "native Apple" of the five.
02

Playful Dock

Mini app-icon tiles in candy gradients on a glass shelf — real dock magnification as your mouse sweeps, tooltips above, running-dot under the active tool. The most playful.
03

Segmented Lens

Three separate glass segments — draw / ink / time — with an iOS-springy sliding lens. Cleanest information grouping, most compact height.
04

Floating Islands

Tinted glass islets (neutral draw, warm ink, cool history) with tiny uppercase labels floating above. Buttons wiggle-tilt on hover; islands lift as a group.
05

Candy Bubble

Round glass bubbles in one clear bar — the active tool inflates in its own candy color with a soft glow, keycaps pop in on hover. Maximum personality per pixel.
